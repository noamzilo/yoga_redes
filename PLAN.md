# Majo's plan: students from videos

> **The biggest risk is stopping.** Doing it every day is very likely to work.

## Goal

- Leads: people who write on WhatsApp and pay for a month. The first lesson of the month is free.
- Path: video → link → WhatsApp → pays for a month → you ask for a referral.

## 1. Configure everything (first)

- Open YouTube, TikTok (as a **Business account**, so the bio link works), Facebook page. Same name as Instagram (yoga.udara).
- Every bio: **"👇 Tu primera clase de regalo"** above the WhatsApp link.
- Instagram and Facebook: add the WhatsApp button.
- One WhatsApp link per platform, to know where each lead came from:
  `https://wa.me/549XXXXXXXXXX?text=Hola%20Majo%2C%20vengo%20de%20YouTube`
- Instagram and Facebook: in Meta Business Suite, set "comment to message" for the word **SALUD**. It sends the link.
- Instagram: a Story highlight "Clase de regalo" with a link sticker to WhatsApp.
- The offer: one month, your price, first lesson free. Write your first WhatsApp answer so it says this clearly.
- Buy a clip-on microphone. Install CapCut.
- Later, not now: WhatsApp auto-reply, a website.

## 2. Build the buffer (work up front)

- Plan 7 connected ideas (one series), each one checked (see Ideas). Write a short outline for each.
- Record and edit all 7. Schedule them.
- Only then publish the first one.

## 3. Every day

- 1 long video (20 to 30 min) and 3 shorts cut in CapCut, on all 4 platforms.
- Every video has a clickable link (see Links).
- Answer SALUD comments and WhatsApp.
- About 1 hour 30 min to 2 hours.
- Work in sprints: film 3 or 4 videos in one session, edit them in another.
- Keep 7 or more videos scheduled. Under 4: your next free day is a filming sprint. This keeps uploads going when you are sick or tired.
- After each series of 7: fill the sheet. The next series copies the angle that won.

## The videos

- Formats: a guided session, one topic in depth, or a list ("4 ejercicios para..."). Lists have the best backing: physio channels with 21,000 to 102,000 subscribers got 9 to 30 times their size in views.
- Start: "Hola, soy Majo, licenciada en kinesiología."
- Show the pose, say its name, do it, narrate while you do it.
- Call to action at the start and the end, said out loud and as text at the end: "Comentá SALUD o tocá el link: tu primera clase de regalo."
- Never promise healing: "Si tenés una lesión, consultá a un profesional."
- A pose can come back after 2 weeks.
- Shorts of lying poses: show the whole frame, with text above and below, so your body is not cut off.

## Titles

- Specific: one pose, one problem, or one situation. That is what people in pain type.
- Evergreen: still useful in years. No dates, trends or "2026". Search keeps sending people for a long time.
- Bad: "Yoga para el dolor de espalda" (huge channels own it). Good: "4 ejercicios para la ciática en el embarazo".

## Ideas

- Record an idea only if a video on it did much better than expected: a channel under 20,000 subscribers with 3 times more views than subscribers, or under 200,000 with 5 times more.
- 7 connected ideas per series: same problem, different angles. Then you can compare them.
- To check: open Claude Code in the `yoga_redes` folder and write, for example, "Revisá estos títulos: 1. ... 2. ..." or "Buscame 7 ideas conectadas sobre dolor lumbar".
- The answer: GO, CHANGE (with a better title), or SKIP, plus 3 title ideas. Saved in `research/`.
- Change the checker the same way: "Considerá chico un canal de menos de 50.000 suscriptores."
- Found so far: wide topics (dolor lumbar, cervicales) belong to channels with 1.8 to 3.7 million subscribers. Narrow topics (ciática en el embarazo) and lists work for small channels.

## Links: every video has one you can click

- YouTube long video: first line of the description, and a pinned comment.
- YouTube Shorts: links do not work. Set the long video as the "related video".
- Facebook: in the description.
- Instagram: no links in Reel text. Share each Reel to your Story with a link sticker. SALUD sends the link.
- TikTok: no links in video text. The bio link, and reply to SALUD by hand.
- SALUD comments also help the video spread.

## Tricks

- Always ask: "¿Conocés a alguien más a quien le pueda servir?"
- Offer yoga to your kinesiology patients.

## What the results mean

- Views but no messages: the call to action or the link.
- SALUD comments but no messages: the automation is broken.
- Messages but nobody pays: the first answer or the offer is not clear.
- Students do not renew: the lessons.
- Few views after 60 videos: titles too wide.

## The sheet: one row per video

- Series, title, date, views after 7 days per platform, SALUD comments, messages per platform, students who paid.
