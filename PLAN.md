# Majo's plan: getting students from videos

> **The biggest risk is stopping.** Views stay low at the start, and that is when people give up.
> **Doing it every day is very likely to work:** it is very likely to bring you messages and your first students.

## The goal

The only goal right now is **leads**: people who write to you on WhatsApp and then pay for a month.

The path a new student takes:

1. Finds your video (search on YouTube, or Shorts / Reels / TikTok).
2. Hears you say the call to action, and sees it as text at the end.
3. Clicks the link (on the video, in your profile, or in the message SALUD sends them).
4. Writes to you on WhatsApp (later: a website).
5. Pays for a month. The first lesson of the month is free.
6. After the lessons, you ask: "¿Conocés a alguien más a quien le pueda servir?"

## What to do, in order

1. **Open every platform and put the call to action and the link in every bio.** Before anything else. (See "Bios".)
2. **Decide the offer:** one month of classes, at a price you decide. The first lesson of the month is free. Write your first WhatsApp answer so it says this clearly.
3. **Buy a clip-on microphone** for your phone. Clear voice matters more than the camera.
4. **Make one WhatsApp link per platform** and **set up the SALUD automation** on Instagram and Facebook. (See "Tricks" and "The call to action".)
5. **Plan the first series:** 7 connected ideas, each one checked and backed, each with a short outline. (See "Ideas".)
6. **Record one test video and test the tools:** cut it into shorts, check the crop, schedule it everywhere, check the link works on every platform. (See "Tools".)
7. **Record the first 7 videos** (the buffer) and schedule them.
8. **Start publishing:** one long video and 3 shorts every day, on all 4 platforms, every one with its link.
9. **Keep the buffer at 7 or more** with filming and editing sprints.
10. **After each series of 7:** fill the sheet, see which angle won, and plan the next 7 ideas around the winner.

Not now, later: a WhatsApp Business auto-reply, and a simple website with one button, "Empezá con tu primera clase de regalo" (Noam builds it).

## The link on every video

**Every video has a clickable link in some form.** Put it on the video itself (in the description) wherever the platform allows it.

| Platform | Clickable link on the video | Where it is not possible, the other way |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube long video | Yes: first line of the description. Also a pinned comment with the link. | |
| YouTube Shorts | Not in the description (YouTube's rule since 2023). | Add the long video as the Short's "related video": it is clickable, and the long video has the link. |
| Facebook | Yes: the link in the description. Check it in the tools test. | |
| Instagram Reels | Not in the description, for normal accounts. | Share every Reel to your Story with a **link sticker** to WhatsApp. Keep a Story highlight "Clase de regalo" with the link. Plus the bio link and the SALUD message. |
| TikTok | Not in the description. | The bio link. Plus SALUD comments, answered by hand. |

## The call to action

The same video goes to all 4 platforms, so the spoken call to action must work on all of them.

**Say it** (start and end): "Comentá SALUD y te paso cómo empezar. O tocá el link. La primera clase es de regalo."

**Show it** as text in the last seconds: "Comentá SALUD o tocá el link: tu primera clase de regalo".

SALUD comments do two jobs: they help the video spread (comments show the platform that people care), and they are one more way to reach the link where the platform blocks links.

| Platform | What happens after someone comments SALUD |
|---|---|
| Instagram | Automatic and free: Meta Business Suite > Inbox > Automations > "comment to message", keyword SALUD. They get a private message with the WhatsApp link. (Desktop. Menu names may differ.) |
| Facebook | The same automation, for the page |
| TikTok | TikTok has no "comment, get a message" feature. Reply by hand: "¡Hola! El link está en mi perfil 🙌" |
| YouTube long videos | YouTube has no private messages. Reply by hand with the link (links in long-video comments are clickable). |
| YouTube Shorts | Reply by hand: "El link está en mi perfil". Links in Shorts comments are not clickable. |

## The videos

### Long video: 20 to 30 minutes

It can be:

- a guided session ("Yoga para la ciática: clase completa de 25 minutos"),
- one topic in depth ("Postura del niño: todo lo que tenés que saber si te duele la espalda"),
- a list ("5 cosas que empeoran tu dolor lumbar", "4 ejercicios para...").

The list format has strong backing: physiotherapy channels with 21,000 to 102,000 subscribers got 9 to 30 times their size in views with titles like "Lumbalgia: 4 ejercicios para aliviar el dolor lumbar" (see `research/`). These formats need some research and an outline. That time is in the ideas session.

From each long video, cut 3 shorts.

### The recipe for each video (from Evan)

- Show yoga being done. Say the name of the pose. Do it. Narrate what you are doing, while you do it.
- Start with: **"Hola, soy Majo, licenciada en kinesiología."** Say it on camera in every video. It matters more on camera than in the bio.
- Call to action at the start and at the end, or at least at the end.
- One long video per day.
- A pose can come back after 2 weeks.
- Do not promise healing. Add one line: "Si tenés una lesión o un dolor fuerte, consultá a un profesional."

### Titles must be specific and evergreen

**Specific** means one pose, one problem, or one situation. A person in pain types exactly that.

**Evergreen** means the video is still useful in 2 or 3 years: no dates, no trends, no news, no "2026". Search keeps sending people to an evergreen video for months and years. These videos start slowly and grow over time, so most of their views come later.

| Bad title | Why | Good title |
|---|---|---|
| Mi práctica de hoy | Nobody searches for it | Postura del niño si te duele la espalda baja: clase de 20 minutos |
| Yoga para el dolor de espalda | Too wide: channels with millions of subscribers own it | 4 ejercicios para la ciática en el embarazo |
| Rutina de noviembre 2026 | Old next month | Cobra para principiantes: 5 errores que lastiman la espalda |

## Ideas: series of 7 connected ideas, each one backed

**The rule: you record an idea only if a video on the same topic did much better than expected for a small or mid-size channel.** That proves people want it and that a channel without millions of subscribers can get the views. "Much better than expected" means:

- a channel under 20,000 subscribers whose video has 3 times more views than the channel has subscribers, or
- a channel under 200,000 subscribers whose video has 5 times more views than subscribers.

**Connected** means the 7 ideas of one series share one problem or one body area, each with a different angle. Example for "ciática": a full class, 4 exercises, 5 mistakes, ciática in pregnancy, ciática after sitting all day, one pose in depth, a 10-minute morning routine. When the series is out, you compare: which angle got the most views and messages? The next series copies the winner.

### The ideas session (about 1 hour 30 minutes, once per series)

1. Write 10 to 15 titles around one theme (15 min).
2. Check them (below). Keep the 7 that get GO (15 min, mostly waiting).
3. For each of the 7, write a short outline: the poses, the 4 or 5 points, what to say. Watch the backing video for ideas (1 hour).

### How to check ideas

The checker is Claude, an AI assistant, set up in this folder. Open Claude Code in the `yoga_redes` folder and write in normal words, Spanish or English. Examples:

- "Revisá estos títulos: 1. Yoga para la ciática en el embarazo. 2. 5 errores en la postura de la cobra. 3. Clase de 25 minutos para el cuello."
- "Buscame 7 ideas conectadas sobre dolor lumbar, todas respaldadas."
- "¿Vale la pena grabar 'yoga para la fibromialgia'?"

For each title you get **GO** (backed: it names the video that proves it), **CHANGE** (not backed as written, here is a better title that is backed), or **SKIP** (nothing backs it). It also proposes 3 titles. Every check is saved in `research/`.

**You can change how the checker works the same way, in normal words.** For example:

- "A partir de ahora, considerá chico un canal de menos de 50.000 suscriptores."
- "Además de los títulos, proponé el texto para la miniatura."
- "Buscá solo videos de más de 15 minutos."

## Tools: test before publishing

Test with one real 20 to 30 minute video:

1. Cut it into 3 shorts with each tool below. Time it.
2. Check the vertical crop: is your whole body in the frame, in standing, sitting **and** lying poses?
3. Schedule the long video and the 3 shorts on all 4 platforms. Time it.
4. Check the link on every platform: click it from a phone, as a viewer would (see "The link on every video").
5. Pick the fastest way that works. Write the steps down so you repeat them without thinking.

| Job | Tool | What the free version gives (checked Nov 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Schedule YouTube (long and Shorts) | YouTube Studio | Free, built in |
| Schedule Instagram and Facebook | Meta Business Suite | Free, built in |
| Schedule TikTok | TikTok on the computer | Free, built in. Check scheduling in the test. |
| All 4 platforms in one place | Metricool | Free: 1 brand, 20 posts per month. Not enough for daily posting on 4 platforms, so the paid plan would be needed. |
| All 4 platforms in one place | Buffer | Free: 3 channels, 10 posts waiting per channel. Not enough for 4 platforms. |
| Cut a long video into shorts | YouTube "Edit into a Short" | Free. You pick the part. |
| Cut a long video into shorts, automatically | OpusClip | Free: about 60 minutes of video per month, with a watermark, and clips are deleted after 3 days. |
| Edit and add captions | CapCut | Free |

**Why the crop test matters.** A short is vertical. A vertical crop of a horizontal video keeps only about one third of the width. In lying or wide poses, your body gets cut off. If the test fails, choose one:

- make shorts only from standing and sitting poses,
- show the whole horizontal frame inside the vertical short, with text above and below,
- record with a second phone standing vertical at the same time.

## Tricks

1. **Comment SALUD** (see "The call to action").
2. **Ask for referrals, always.** After the lessons: "¿Conocés a alguien más a quien le pueda servir?"
3. **Offer yoga to your kinesiology patients** as a complement to their treatment. They already trust you.
4. **Know where each lead came from.** Use a different WhatsApp link per platform, with a ready message, for example:
   `https://wa.me/549XXXXXXXXXX?text=Hola%20Majo%2C%20vengo%20de%20YouTube`
   (replace the X's with your number; change "YouTube" to "Instagram", "TikTok", "Facebook"). Then every message tells you which platform brought it.

## Daily work

These are estimates. Time yourself when you test the tools.

| Task | Time | How |
|---|---|---|
| Read the outline of today's video | 5 min | Written in the ideas session |
| Set up and record, narrating while you do it | 35 to 45 min | A 20 to 30 minute video plus retakes. No separate voice-over. |
| Cut 3 shorts | 20 to 30 min | The tool that won the test |
| Titles, description with the link, call to action text, scheduling | 15 to 20 min | Copy from a template |
| Answer SALUD comments and WhatsApp | 15 min | |
| **Total** | **about 1 hour 30 min to 2 hours** | Plus the lessons |

Once per series of 7: the ideas session (1 hour 30 min) and the review (15 min).

## Filming and editing sprints

It is easier in sprints than one video at a time:

- **Filming sprint:** record 3 or 4 videos in one session. Same setup, same light, same clothes if you want.
- **Editing sprint:** in another session, cut the shorts, write the titles and descriptions, and schedule all of them.

## The buffer: always 7 videos ahead, or more

Before the first video goes public, have **at least 7 videos ready and scheduled** (one series).

- Always keep 7 or more in the queue.
- If the queue drops under 4, your next free day is a filming sprint.
- Why: when you are sick, tired, or busy, the uploads keep happening. Missing days is how this plan dies.

## What the results mean

| What happens | What is wrong | What to change |
|---|---|---|
| Views but no SALUD comments and no WhatsApp messages | The call to action or the link | Say it more clearly, make the link easier to find, check the link works on every platform |
| SALUD comments but no WhatsApp messages | The automation or the link it sends | Test the SALUD automation yourself from another account |
| Messages, but nobody pays for a month | The first answer or the offer | Answer faster, explain clearly what the month includes and that the first lesson is free |
| Students pay but do not renew | The lessons | Fix the lessons. The videos are not the problem. |
| Almost no views after 60 videos | Titles too wide | Narrower titles: one pose, one problem, one situation. Check more ideas. |

## The sheet: one row per video

Series, title, date, views after 7 days on each platform, SALUD comments, WhatsApp messages (by platform, from the tracking links), students who paid. After each series, compare its 7 videos: the angle that won decides the next series.
