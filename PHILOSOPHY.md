# Philosophy: starting a yoga channel and getting leads from it

> **The biggest risk is an empty buffer**, more than not filming every day. You do not have to film every day. A video has to go out every day.
> Publishing every day is very likely to work.

## Goal

- Leads: people who write on WhatsApp and pay for a month. The first lesson of the month is free.
- Path: video → link → WhatsApp → pays for a month → you ask for a referral.

## 1. Configure everything (first, once)

- Open YouTube, TikTok (as a **Business account**, so the bio link works), Facebook page. Same name as Instagram (yoga.udara).
- Every bio: **"👇 Tu primera clase privada de regalo"** above the WhatsApp link.
- Instagram and Facebook: add the WhatsApp button.
- One WhatsApp link per platform, to know where each lead came from:
  `https://wa.me/549XXXXXXXXXX?text=Hola%20Majo%2C%20vengo%20de%20YouTube`
- Instagram and Facebook: in Meta Business Suite, set "comment to message" for the word **SALUD**. It sends the link.
- Instagram: a Story highlight "Clase privada" with a link sticker to WhatsApp.
- The offer: one month, your price, first lesson free. Write your first WhatsApp answer so it says this clearly.
- Buy a clip-on microphone. Install CapCut.
- Later, not now: WhatsApp auto-reply, a website.

## 2. Plan the week (before every week)

- One theme per week, 7 connected videos: same problem, different angles (a full class, a list, one pose in depth...).
- The next week: a different theme.
- Every idea is found and validated with Claude Code (see the end). Then you can compare the 7, and the angle that won comes back in a later theme.
- Write a short outline for each video.

## 3. Build the buffer (work up front)

- Record and edit the whole first week. Schedule it.
- Only then publish the first video.
- From then on, stay at least one week ahead. When the buffer drops under 4 videos, filming comes first.

## 4. Every day

- 1 long video (20 to 30 min) and 3 shorts cut in CapCut, on all 4 platforms.
- Every video has a clickable link (see Links).
- Answer SALUD comments and WhatsApp.
- About 1 hour 30 min to 2 hours.
- Suggestion: film 3 or 4 videos in one session and edit them in another. It is often faster.
- After each week: fill the sheet.

## The videos

- Formats: a guided session, one topic in depth, or a list ("4 ejercicios para..."). Lists have the best backing: physio channels with 21,000 to 102,000 subscribers got 9 to 30 times their size in views. Search and validate more ideas like this with Claude Code.
- First sentence: what they will get, in the same words as the title and the caption. Title "4 ejercicios para la escoliosis" → "En este video te muestro 4 ejercicios para la escoliosis." Then: "Soy Majo, licenciada en kinesiología."
- Show the pose, say its name, do it, narrate while you do it.
- Call to action at the start and the end, said out loud and as text at the end: "Comentá SALUD o tocá el link: tu primera clase privada de regalo."
- Never promise healing: "Si tenés una lesión, consultá a un profesional."
- A pose can come back after 2 weeks.
- Shorts of lying poses: look at how other yoga channels cut them from their long videos (they do not film them separately), and copy it.

## Titles

- Specific: one pose, one problem, or one situation. That is what people in pain type.
- Evergreen: still useful in years. No dates, trends or "2026". Search keeps sending people for a long time.
- Bad: "Yoga para el dolor de espalda" (huge channels own it). Good: "Yoga para la escoliosis: 4 ejercicios".

## Links: every video has one you can click

- YouTube long video: first line of the description, and a pinned comment.
- YouTube Shorts: links do not work. Set the long video as the "related video".
- Facebook: in the description.
- Instagram: no links in Reel text. Share each Reel to your Story with a link sticker. SALUD sends the link.
- TikTok: no links in video text. The bio link, and reply to SALUD by hand.
- SALUD comments also help the video spread.

## What the results mean

- Views but no messages: the call to action or the link.
- SALUD comments but no messages: the automation is broken.
- Messages but nobody pays: the first answer or the offer is not clear.
- Students do not renew: the lessons.
- Few views after 60 videos: titles too wide.

## The sheet: one row per video

- Week (theme), title, date, views after 7 days per platform, SALUD comments, messages per platform, students who paid.

## Ideas: what works, who to learn from, and how to find more

**The rule:** record an idea only if a video on it did much better than expected: a channel under 20,000 subscribers with 3 times more views than subscribers, or under 200,000 with 5 times more.

**Yoga + kinesiology topics that worked for small channels** (checked 8 Nov 2026):

- Escoliosis: "Ejercicios de yoga para escoliosis", Saulo Rafael: 1,770 subscribers, 46,910 views. "ESCOLIOSIS: Ejercicios Correctivos", FT. Laura Becerra (physiotherapist): 3,120 subscribers, 38,065 views.
- Artrosis de cadera: "YOGA para ARTRITIS y ARTROSIS de CADERA. Vídeo 1", LanaVedas: 6,790 subscribers, 94,546 views. Video 2 of the series: 42,433. A connected series, like your weeks.
- Fascitis plantar: "Fascitis plantar: alivia con yoga", Yoga con Caro: 523 subscribers, 13,322 views.
- Osteoporosis: "12 posturas para huesos fuertes", Abhyasa Yoga Shala: 3,300 subscribers, 16,954 views.
- Columna: "Yoga sano para la columna vertebral. Sesión completa", Yoga Art Studio: 24,200 subscribers, 832,537 views (65 min).
- Lumbalgia: "Lumbalgia: 4 ejercicios para aliviar el dolor lumbar", Fisioincorpore: 21,200 subscribers, 192,284 views.
- Avoid the wide versions: dolor de hombro, artrosis de rodilla, postura encorvada, hernia lumbar, rectificación cervical. Big channels own them. Look for a narrower angle.

**Channels to learn from** (small or mid-size, beat their size): Saulo Rafael, FT. Laura Becerra, LanaVedas, Yoga con Caro, Fisioincorpore, Yoga Art Studio, Carmina Yoga, Lina Alma Zen, Clínica de la Columna (Dr. Miguel Santana).

**The competition** (millions of subscribers): Elena Malova, Xuan Lan Yoga, Anabel Otero (also "Yoga Terapéutico"), Marcos Sacristán (fisioterapia), PhysioWods.

**Find and validate more with Claude Code, the idea checker.** Open Claude Code in the `yoga_redes` folder and write in normal words:

- "Revisá estos títulos: 1. ... 2. ..."
- "Buscame 7 ideas conectadas sobre escoliosis, todas respaldadas."
- "¿Cómo cortan los shorts de posturas acostadas otros canales de yoga?"

It answers GO, CHANGE (with a better title) or SKIP for each idea, names the video that backs it, and saves everything in `research/`. You can change how it works the same way, for example: "Considerá chico un canal de menos de 50.000 suscriptores."
